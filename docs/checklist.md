# The Verification Checklist

A printable one-pager. Tape it near the screen, tuck it in the notebook,
or paste it into the family chat's pinned messages. (Expandable answers
from the drills are hidden in print — this page is all signal.)

## Before you even send the ask

- [ ] I asked *which row* this is: none pays, I pay a little, I pay a lot, someone else pays
- [ ] Rows 1–2: skim and use freely — no further checks required
- [ ] Row 3: on the way to verify-before-trust below
- [ ] Row 4 (legal/medical/money/official): AI is my copilot; a licensed human decides
- [ ] My question is neutrally worded (no leading "right?")
- [ ] I named my specifics in the prompt (state, device, situation)

## Reading the answer

- [ ] I read once for usefulness first
- [ ] I identified the load-bearing details — names, numbers, dates, citations, instructions
- [ ] There are five or fewer of them (more suggests a row-4 moment)
- [ ] I noticed if anything is rare, recent, or numeric (the three fingertip flags)
- [ ] I noticed the absolutes: "always," "never," "everyone," "proven"

## The fact checks (rows above line)

- [ ] I searched any quoted/cited claim in exact quotation marks
- [ ] I recomputed whatever arithmetic is load-bearing
- [ ] I checked date-sensitive claims against the live source
- [ ] I opened/clicked any link the AI gave me — it loaded and says it
- [ ] I confirmed procedure steps against the actual screen
- [ ] The primary source said the same thing, not just another AI

## The second and third opinions

- [ ] I asked a second model — different family, same neutral question
- [ ] I compared only the load-bearing details, not the prose
- [ ] Differences got a real source check, not a coin flip
- [ ] For anything I'll repeat in public: three independent sources
- [ ] Sources that contradict → I said "unresolved" instead of picking

## Never-delegate guardrails

- [ ] No dose, symptom-triage, or treatment decision came from AI
- [ ] No "what do I sign/file/plead" verdict came from AI
- [ ] No invest/withdraw/qualify verdict came from AI
- [ ] Personal or medical details stayed out of searches where possible
- [ ] The professional got my prepared questions, not my AI's answers

## The deciding rules

- [ ] "Who pays if wrong?" sorted this — and I said the row out loud
- [ ] Confirming edge: unresolved is a legitimate verdict
- [ ] I caught one early — my correction beat my silence
- [ ] If money and a stranger are involved, I opened *The AI Scam Safety Guide*
- [ ] If media (image/voice/video) is the thing being faked, I opened *The Spot Fake Media Guide*
- [ ] One rung of diagnosis — not a personality audit — and then I let it go
- [ ] I fixed the process where it was actually thin, in one small change
